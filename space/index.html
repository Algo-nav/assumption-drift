Known limitations

  1. This covers twelve companies. It is a pilot, not a survey of the market.
  2. The pipeline does not claim to capture every guidance statement a company ever made; it captures the ones its patterns and its model caught.
  3. A row reported better than guided is recorded as missed, the same as one reported worse, because the guided range was wrong either way. Which side it fell on is reported separately, not folded into the status. Whether a side is good or bad is fixed once per metric, the same for every company: operating expenses and the tax rate are treated as lower-is-better, everything else as higher-is-better.
  4. A one-sided floor ("at least X") resolves as met on any reported value above the floor. There is no ceiling to measure against, so a floor's better-than-guided rows are not measured; only values below it can be missed.
  5. The search for an acknowledgement covers 8-K, 10-K and 10-Q filing text only. A company that only addressed a miss on an earnings call, and never wrote it into a filing, is not found: that text is not in EDGAR.
  6. An outcome that would come from a multi-column table whose header could not be confidently matched to a period is held back as unresolved rather than guessed at.
  7. An acknowledgement is a sentence written after the outcome was reported that refers back to the guided figure. A revision made while the period was still open, such as 'increased from our prior outlook of $60-65 billion', is a new guidance row, not an acknowledgement. A later release will record these revisions separately; when it does, the acknowledged counts will change.

Coverage notes

  1. Lowe's and Home Depot: most margin and tax rate rows are unresolved because those companies do not print the reported figure as a line in the release; it must be derived from two reported lines. These will resolve in a later release.
  2. Qualcomm: guidance from early 2019 to late 2020 is not yet included because of a two-column table layout the parser does not read. Coverage starts at Q1 FY2021.
  3. Adobe: the revenue and EPS targets table is not yet parsed; Adobe rows are non-GAAP operating margin only.
  4. Microsoft: guidance appeared in filing text only from August 2024; earlier guidance was given on earnings calls, which are not in EDGAR.
  5. Broadcom: adjusted EBITDA guidance is not a tracked metric; rows are revenue and, from 2026, non-GAAP operating margin.
  6. Micron is reviewed separately and not in this release.
  7. Apple, Alphabet, Costco and JPMorgan issue no numeric guidance in filing text.
